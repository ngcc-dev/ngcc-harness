## Attacks considered

- sign-02-1: A 512-bit unsalted message representative caps forgery security at 256 bits (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-02-2: A shared bimodal sign leaks an equivalent BiT-128 signing key (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-02-3: Mask-seed collisions recover an equivalent BiT-128 signing key (Design, Probable)

## Remediation

- **sign-02-1.** Root cause: BiT-512 signs the fixed 512-bit digest µ = H(tr ‖ M), so two messages with one µ share every signature. The costed repair is the submitters' proposal, as recorded in the report and not evaluated: the message and its encoded length enter the Fiat–Shamir challenge hash in place of the prehash. It changes the challenge derivation of Figure 5, Sign lines 4 and 11 and Verify lines 4 and 8, and Sign line 7 where µ also seeds the masks. Scope: Function, specification and code. Impact: none. µ is hashed and never sent, and the signature stays c̃ ‖ z1,0 ‖ z1,1 ‖ h at 6,695 bytes. For a message of up to 64 bytes each challenge hash absorbs at most 72 bytes more than the submitted 1,984, beside 25,600 bytes of mask expansion per full resample, and the separate µ hash goes away. A long message is then absorbed in every signing attempt, about three at the stated 33% acceptance rate, where the submitted code hashes it once; the report does not record how the revision orders the hash inputs. For long messages the figure therefore rests on the cheaper standard repair, widening µ to 1,024 bits, which hashes the message once and also adds no byte. The contest hash of the default build has a 1,024-bit digest, and the SHA3-512 of the optional SHAKE build already has a 1,024-bit capacity, so the hashing rate stays as it is. The revised specification was not read.
- **sign-02-2.** Root cause: one sign bit b is shared by the whole response, while RejectSample corrects each coefficient against a scalar mixture (Figures 3 and 4), so the joint response keeps correlations that depend on the key. The costed repair is the submitters' proposal, as recorded in the report and not evaluated: a parabolic rejection sampler over the whole response vector in place of coefficientwise rejection, and the complete public key b in place of its high part b1. A repair that replaces the rejection step and keeps the compressed key is not taken as credible, because the finding records the submitters' statement that the compressed key leaks as well and their proposal removes the compression. Scope: Parameter set, specification and code: the rejection step is replaced, and the acceptance rate of Lemma 1 and the mask widths chosen from it (Table 9) have to be derived again for the new rule. The public-key encoding changes. Impact: public key +39% at BiT-128, +54% at BiT-256 and +46% at BiT-512 (count), with b packed at ⌈log2 q⌉ bits per coefficient. The effect of the new rejection step on the signature size and on the expected signing attempts is not estimated and is not in these figures: the report records no parameters for it, and no model for it is available here. For scale only, one more bit per coefficient of z1 is 7.4% to 8.5% of the signature (count). Not checked: the revised specification and code; whether the revision still sends the complete public key; the leak through the compressed key, for which the report has no witness.
- **sign-02-3.** Root cause: at BiT-128 the 128-bit seed_y alone determines the masks of a signing call, so two calls with equal seeds on different messages reveal s0. A stated limit of 2^64 signatures per key is not a repair, because the report gives 10.3% success at 2^64 signatures. The costed repair widens seed_y, and the randomness rnd that it is derived from, to 2κ = 256 bits at BiT-128, the usual repair of a narrow secret seed; under the finding's model the expected number of usable pairs at the 2^80 budget is then 2^-99, and 205 bits is the smallest width that keeps the attack at 2^128 there. rnd widens with the seed because seed_y = XOF(ρ′ ‖ rnd ‖ µ) takes only as many values as rnd when one message is signed again, while the sign bit b is drawn separately; the finding's difference equations then hold with c = c′ and b ≠ b′. The report's witness uses different messages and does not record this case. Scope: Function, specification and code: the lengths in Sign lines 6 and 7 of Figure 5. In the code the mask seed and rnd need their own length, because `BIT_SEEDBYTES` also sizes the public seed ρ. Impact: none. Neither value is sent; signing draws 16 more random bytes, and each mask and rejection stream absorbs 16 more bytes. Raising the shared constant instead would add 16 bytes to the public key (+1.5%). The report records no proposed fix for this finding. BiT-256 and BiT-512 are outside the finding and are not changed.
