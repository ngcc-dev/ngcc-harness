Yes: enlarging the rejection bound `beta1` until `c*s1` does not exceed it (sign-15-7) multiplies the expected signing attempts by about 1.9 to 2.0 at ATLAS-128 and 2.5 to 3.6 at ATLAS-192, and the heavier ATLAS-512 challenge (sign-15-8) adds 17% to 23% more attempts there, while no transmitted object grows by more than 0.1%.

Impact: signing +34% to +192% at ATLAS-192 (estimate); signature +0.07% at ATLAS-512 (count)
Scope: Parameter set (large), specification and code
Assessed: 2026-11-23

## Attacks considered

- sign-15-3: The ATLAS-192 challenge space is below 192 bits (Implementation, Confirmed)
- sign-15-4: Repeated masking coefficients expose the signing key (Implementation, Confirmed)
- sign-15-7: Finite mask support leaks the specified ATLAS-128 signing key (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-15-8: The specified ATLAS-512 challenge space is only 322.67 bits (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-15-9: A 384-bit message representative caps ATLAS-256 and -512 forgery security at 192 bits (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-15-10: A 256-bit mask key and key-generation seed cap ATLAS-512 (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-15-12: A byte-wide position index shrinks the ATLAS-512 challenge image (Implementation, Confirmed)
- sign-15-13: Overlapping mask streams recover the ATLAS-512 signing key (Implementation, Confirmed)
- sign-15-14: Verification hashes one message and returns another (Implementation, Confirmed)

## Remediation

- **sign-15-3.** The ATLAS-192 code uses `kappa=64` where Table 2 (physical p. 22) has 69, because the challenge signs are held in one 64-bit word (`lwrdsa192/params.h:68`; `SIG_lwrdsa192.c:110,128–130,155–156`; `lwrdsa192/packing.c:154,187`), and `challenge()` replays its buffer (`SIG_lwrdsa192.c:138–149`). Repair: `kappa=69`, a 9-byte sign field, and a sampler that continues the XOF stream. The specification's signature formula also allows only 8 sign bytes (`n/8 + 8`, Table 3, physical p. 23), so the stated size changes with the code. Scope: Function, specification and code; the signature encoding changes. Impact: signature +1 byte at ATLAS-192, 3365 to 3366, +0.03% (count). Speed: none; `pol_mul` is a dense multiplication, so the weight of `c` does not change its cost.
- **sign-15-4.** `rej_gamma1m1` overwrites its first 20-bit value and stores the second one twice (`lwrdsa128/poly.c:506–515`, `lwrdsa192/poly.c:517–526`, `lwrdsa256/poly.c:554–563`). Repair: store both values. Scope: Function, code. The XOF bytes drawn do not change. The corrected code rejects more often, because two coefficients that share one mask value pass the `|z| < gamma1 - beta1` check together more often than two independent ones. Impact: expected attempts +3% at ATLAS-128 and ATLAS-192 (the latter with `kappa=69`) and +11% at ATLAS-256; signing +3% to +7% at ATLAS-256 (estimate). This effect is modelled, not measured.
- **sign-15-7.** Root cause: `beta1` is far below the reach of `c*s1`, 51 against `kappa*eta = 496` at ATLAS-128 and 33 against 552 at ATLAS-192, so the accepted `z` depends on `s1`. Repair: enlarge `beta1`, which the specification itself says should be about `kappa*eta` (physical p. 21). It is costed at two values: the smallest `beta1` that no coefficient of `c*s1` is expected to exceed in 2^80 signatures (457 and 406), and `kappa*eta`. Nothing else moves, so no size changes and the bounds `zeta` and `zeta'` (physical p. 25) stay as they are. Scope: Parameter set, specification and code. Impact against the submitted code, with the sign-15-3 and sign-15-4 corrections included: expected attempts ×1.86 to ×1.97 at ATLAS-128 and ×2.5 to ×3.6 at ATLAS-192; signing +24% to +67% at ATLAS-128 and +34% to +192% at ATLAS-192 (estimate). The low ends count XOF bytes and the high ends count multiplications. Key generation, verification and all sizes: none. The ATLAS-192 figures rest on the finding's Probable extension, and 192 bits is not a level of the call; if ATLAS-192 were withdrawn, the ATLAS-128 figures decide. Alternatives, not costed: a larger `gamma1` buys attempts back at `n*l/8` signature bytes per doubling (96 bytes, +4.6%, at ATLAS-128). One doubling leaves ×1.37 attempts. Two doublings (+9.2%) leave ×1.14, but then `zeta' = 2*gamma1 + 2` passes `q/2`, and the specification fixes `gamma1 = q/16` (physical p. 21), so `q` and the estimates of §3.3 would have to move. A smaller `eta` would also satisfy `kappa*eta <= beta1` at ATLAS-128, but the specification derives `eta = q/(2p)` (physical p. 21), the key distribution and the MLWR estimate would change, and it cannot work at ATLAS-192, where `kappa > beta1`. ATLAS-256 and -512 (`beta1 = 73` against `kappa*eta = 240`) are not claimed by the finding and are not costed.
- **sign-15-8.** Root cause: `kappa=60` at `n=512` gives 2^322.67 challenges. Repair: `kappa=118`, the smallest weight that gives 2^512, as the finding states. Three values follow from it. The sign field grows from 8 to 15 bytes. `beta2` rises from 12 to 17 to keep the specification's Event3 rule (physical p. 18) at the submitted level, because `c*s2` grows with the square root of `kappa`; the specification's own 384-bit set pairs `kappa=133` with `beta2=18` (physical p. 48). `beta1 = 73` would be exceeded by 0.9% of `c*s1` coefficients instead of 0.03%, which is the mechanism of sign-15-7, so the upper figures scale `beta1` to 103 to keep the submitted rate. Scope: Parameter set, specification and code; the signature encoding changes. Impact: signature +7 bytes at ATLAS-512, 10081 to 10088, +0.07% (count); expected attempts ×1.17 to ×1.23; signing +6% to +14% (estimate), a range that straddles 10%. With `beta1 = kappa*eta = 472` the attempts would be ×2.3. Checked: `|c*t0|` and the expected hint weight (40 against `omega=128`) stay inside their bounds. Not checked: `zeta` rises by 10%, still below the `4*gamma2` that the specification states, and the lattice estimates were not redone.
- **sign-15-9.** Root cause: `mu = CRH(tr || M)` is 48 bytes at every level (`CRHBYTES`, `params.h:10`; physical p. 6). Repair: a 64-byte output at ATLAS-256 and a 128-byte output at ATLAS-512. `mu` is hashed and never sent. Scope: Function, specification and code. Impact: none; the challenge hash absorbs 16 or 80 more bytes per attempt, against 1,024 or 2,048 bytes of packed `w1`. If `tr` is widened with `mu`, the secret key grows by 16 or 80 bytes. The cost per message byte is taken as unchanged, on the assumption that the placeholder XOF is replaced by a primitive sized for the level, which the challenge hash needs in any case (judgment).
- **sign-15-10.** Root cause: `K` is 256 bits (Algorithm 1, physical p. 13), and the code derives `rho`, the `s1` seed and `K` from one 32-byte draw (`SIG_lwrdsa512.c:191–206`). Repair at ATLAS-512: a 64-byte `K`, and a 64-byte key-generation root and `s1` seed. All three are secret; `rho` in the public key stays. Scope: Function, specification and code. Impact: none on transmitted objects; the secret key grows from 7920 to 8032 bytes together with sign-15-9 (+1.4%, count). Each mask XOF call absorbs 112 more bytes, 32 for `K` and 80 for the wider `mu` of sign-15-9: under 1% of the signing XOF bytes with one stream per polynomial, about 5% if the code keeps one call per 816-byte block (estimate).
- **sign-15-12.** `challenge()` reads one byte per position where Algorithm 14 (physical p. 40) draws from `0..i` with `i` up to 511 (`SIG_lwrdsa512.c:109,151–152`). Repair: draw at least 9 bits per position. Scope: Function, code. Impact: none; a few hundred XOF bytes per attempt against about 45,700 for the masks.
- **sign-15-13.** The signer and the mask sampler both advance one nonce (`SIG_lwrdsa512.c:326`, `lwrdsa512/poly.c:714–734`). Repair: separate the polynomial index from the block counter. Scope: Function, code. Impact: none; the same number of blocks is drawn.
- **sign-15-14.** `sig_verify` hashes a zero-filled buffer when `sn == m`, then copies `sn[CRYPTO_BYTES..]` into `m` (`SIG_lwrdsa128.c:475–477,525–526`; `SIG_lwrdsa512.c:480–482,530–531`). Repair: hash the message that is accepted and returned, and read nothing past the signature. Scope: Function, code. Impact: none; the same number of bytes is hashed.

## Evidence

- Baseline sizes, Table 1 (physical p. 5), for ATLAS-128, -192, -256, -512: public key 1328, 2112, 2848, 6688; secret key 2128, 3152, 4016, 7920; signature 2081, 3365, 4656, 10081 bytes. The Table 3 formulas (physical p. 23) with Table 2 (physical p. 22) reproduce all of them except the ATLAS-512 signature, which is 10080 by the formula; `lwrdsa512/params.h:165` adds one byte.
- Signature: `c` is sent as `n/8` position bytes and 8 sign bytes (`lwrdsa512/packing.c:247–262`). 69 signs need 9 bytes and 118 need 15. log2(C(512,117)·2^117) = 509.44 and log2(C(512,118)·2^118) = 512.18.
- Baseline speed: Table 11 (physical p. 45) gives cycle counts only, with no breakdown, so the counts below are the evidence. By the reference averages there, signing costs 2.5 to 3.0 times key generation (2.0 to 2.6 by the medians). Key generation does the same matrix expansion and `k*l` multiplications as signing does before its loop, and at ATLAS-128 the model below puts signing at 1.4 times its fixed part by XOF bytes (62,849 against 45,360) and 5.6 times by multiplications (301.3 against 54), so both kinds of work matter.
- Attempt model (physical pp. 17–20). Event1: a coefficient is accepted for the mask values that lie in the support `[-(gamma1-1), gamma1-1]` and in the window `|y + v| < gamma1 - beta1`, where `v` is the coefficient of `c*s1`, taken as normal with variance `kappa*E[s^2]`. When two coefficients share one mask value, as in the submitted code for `gamma1 = 2^19`, the values excluded number `max(beta1 - min, 0) + max(beta1 + max, 0)`. The submitted code draws `s1` from `[1, 16]` at ATLAS-128 and `[-7, 8]` at ATLAS-192 (sign-15-16); the nonzero mean gives neighbouring coefficients of `c*s1` a common term, and the model includes it. The corrected rows use the specified ranges. Event2 accepts `2*(gamma2bar - beta2) - 1` of `2*gamma2bar` values. Events 3 to 5 are taken as certain.
- Work model (`SIG_lwrdsa256.c:303–422`): `k*l` multiplications and `k*l` matrix entries once; per attempt `k*l + l` multiplications, `k` more after the `z` check, `k` more after the `r0` check, `l` mask polynomials and one challenge hash. XOF output is 840 bytes per matrix entry (2,520 at ATLAS-512), 816 per mask polynomial (about 8 × 816 at ATLAS-512, where one candidate in four is accepted, `lwrdsa512/poly.c:666–673`), and 136 per challenge.
