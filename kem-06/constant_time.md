# Constant-time review: BRA

The PKE secret `y` (recovered from `sk`), decoded message `m`, fallback `sigma`, and shared secret are secret. The ciphertext and embedded public key are public. This review follows BRA-128; the decoder is duplicated in BRA-256/512, and `augmented_gabidulin.c`, `qpoly.c`, `parsing.c` and `rbc_vec.c` are byte-identical in the optimized tree at all three levels. The finding is a source-level leakage trace, not a demonstrated key-recovery attack.

`src/kem.c:214` decrypts the ciphertext with `sk`, and `src/bra.c:277-288` forms `v-u*y` before augmented-Gabidulin decoding. In `src/augmented_gabidulin.c:184-205`, the pivot `next` is computed from decoded-word discrepancies and then used directly in `u0[next]` and `u1[next]` loads and stores. The memory address can therefore depend on the recipient secret and chosen ciphertext. `src/qpoly.c:580` (`rbc_qpoly_left_div2`, called at `augmented_gabidulin.c:272`) also has a degree-dependent division loop on decoder intermediates; the loop at line 509 belongs to `rbc_qpoly_left_div`, which the decoder does not call. Before decoding, `bra.c:276` re-samples `x` and `y` from the secret seed on every decapsulation (`src/parsing.c:54-78`): the sampler branches on each seed-derived position and coefficient bit (`src/rbc-67/rbc_vec.c:268,287,290`) and the support is drawn in a rejection loop (`rbc_vec.c:184`). By contrast, the final ciphertext comparison and fallback selection at `src/kem.c:228-254` use a fixed-length OR accumulator and masks.

This review does not claim that the pivot trace suffices for key recovery or that a network timing channel is exploitable. The source-level witness is the chain `kem.c:214` → `bra.c:277-288` → `augmented_gabidulin.c:184-205`; see `report.md` for the separate finding.
