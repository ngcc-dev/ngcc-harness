## Attacks considered

- sign-23-1: Reversed transition leaks Shuttle's signing key (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-23-3: A λ-bit masking seed exposes Shuttle signing keys below every claimed level (Design, Probable)

## Remediation

- **sign-23-1.** Root cause: Algorithm 22 sets its flag with the probability that the analysis assigns to returning y − v, and then returns y + flag · v. The costed repair is the submitters' proposal, as recorded in the report and not evaluated: the final update becomes y − flag · v. Scope: Single line, specification and code: Algorithm 22 line 14 and the one update call in each `irs.c`. Impact: none. The corrected step does the same work, one inner product, 15 interval tests and one vector update, τ times per signature. No size changes: the signature length is the fixed sum of Table 8 and the public key holds seedA and b only. The norm gate ‖(z1, z2′)‖2 ≤ Bv and the rANS model are stated for the Gaussian law that Section 4.1.1 derives for the y − v transition, so the stated signing-loop rejection rate of 2^-30 is the one written for the corrected step. Not checked: the restart rate and the rate of rANS reserve overflow of corrected code were not measured, and the revised materials were not read. Section 3.5 speaks of about 2 to 4 signing attempts where Table 10 gives 2^-30; the submitted code's p99/p50 signing ratio of 1.01 to 1.05 agrees with the table.
- **sign-23-3.** Root cause: seed_y is λ bits and alone determines the mask y and the transition coins, so a seed that repeats between two signatures, or that is guessed and tested against the published commitments, yields s1. A stated limit of 2^64 signatures per key is not a repair: at that limit the report gives 0.37 success for the collision route at SHUTTLE-128, and about 2^64, 2^192 and 2^448 seed trials for the multi-target route, all below the targets. The costed repair widens seed_y to 2λ bits (λ/4 bytes), the width that K and seed_sk already have; it is the usual repair of a narrow secret seed. Under the report's model the multi-target route then costs 2^176, 2^432 and 2^944 trials at the 2^80 signature ceiling, and the collision route expects 2^-97 usable pairs at SHUTTLE-128. λ + 80 bits is the smallest width that keeps the multi-target route at 2^λ there. Scope: Single line, specification and code: the length argument of Algorithm 2 line 10, with the seed-length text of Section 2.5.2. In the code the mask seed takes the existing `CHALLENGESEEDBYTES` in place of `SEEDBYTES`. Impact: none. seed_y is in no transmitted object: the signature is seedc ‖ rlen ‖ com ‖ R and the public key is seedA ‖ b1. Each signing attempt squeezes λ/8 more bytes once and absorbs λ/8 more bytes in each of the 16 SampleY contexts and in the transition context. Proposed fixes: the report records none that widens the seed, and it records that the submitters' revised branch keeps λ/8 bytes, so that branch is not relied on. Not checked: the XOF is counted as an ideal one that carries a 2λ-bit seed; the SM3 Hash-DRBG of the default build was not assessed.
