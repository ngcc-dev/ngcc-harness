## Remediation

- **sign-07-2.** The verifier works modulo 2q, where −q·c is the same for +1 and −1 (Algorithm 9 line 2, Algorithm 12 line 8), and the other hash input is LSB(z0 − c). Only the support of c is bound, and Table 3 credits τ sign bits that count for nothing. The costed repair is a heavier challenge, so that binomial(n, τ) alone reaches the level; the specification itself describes a fixed weight over {0, 1} as the usual bimodal challenge (physical p. 4). The smallest weights are τ = 30 at CS-128 (2^129.74, from 23) and τ = 58 at CS-256 (2^256.81, from 44). With every other parameter kept, Theorem 3.2 gives 1.174 and 1.231 times the expected signing attempts and 1.439 and 1.489 times the Reject iterations per signature; no transmitted object changes. On n = 512 no fixed weight reaches 2^512, the largest count being binomial(512, 256) = 2^507.17, so at CS-512 the challenge is costed as all 2^512 binary polynomials, of mean weight 256 against τ = 118. Averaged over the weight, the same model gives 2.49 times the attempts and 3.82 times the Reject iterations; the fixed weight 256, still 4.83 bits short, gives 2.47 and 3.79. Scope: Parameter set, specification and code (τ, the constants M_i, the bounds B0 − N, B1 − τ and B2', the entropy row, Table 4; at CS-512 also the challenge sampler). Impact: signing +16% to +44% at CS-128, +21% to +49% at CS-256 and +136% to +282% at CS-512 (estimate); the low end scales the per-attempt work by the attempts, and the high end is the Reject loop. The other route at CS-512, the next ring dimension with a new modulus and ranks, was not costed. At CS-128 and CS-256 other choices move the cost but keep it in signing. A smaller τ' holds the attempts (×1.03 at CS-128 with τ' = 3, ×0.95 at CS-256 with τ' = 2) and runs 1.69 and 1.89 times the Reject iterations; where its cost falls between those factors depends on the share of the Reject loop in signing, which the counts do not settle. Wider masks hold the attempts and cost 1.19 and 1.22 times the mask bits per attempt, 1.30 times the Reject iterations, and about 17 and 35 signature bytes (+1.1%). With β kept, B2' rises from 2812 to 2917 at CS-128, where Table 4 (physical p. 33) states 128.81 bits for MSIS at B2' and 109.21 at 2B2'; the uncompressed key of sign-07-3 removes the τβ/2 term and gives 2437. At CS-256 B2' rises from 7358 to 8240 against a stated 267.47 bits, and at CS-512 it is 10780 + 15·weight, 12550 as submitted and 14620 at weight 256, against a stated 548.67 bits. The report also asks for a bound on the hint; that is a comparison in verification and adds no bytes and no counted work (judgment; the bound was not derived). The report's other repair, binding the signs into the verifier's equation, is not costed: modulo 2q the term −q·c shows only c mod 2, so it means leaving that relation, a Construction change with no proposal on record.
- **sign-07-3.** The hint is computed from w − 2z2 + 2c_r·b0 (Algorithm 11 line 18), so the value z2' that the verifier recovers contains c_r·b0: the hidden signs times the secret low part of the key relation, outside the rejection step (physical p. 22). The costed repair is to publish b in full, as the specification's simplified scheme does (Algorithm 7, pk = (A', b), physical p. 17). Then b0 = 0 and the term is gone from line 18 and from z2'. No fix is recorded in the report. This repair removes a submitted feature and adds no mechanism; a smaller β only shrinks b0 and leaves the term in place. Scope: Parameter set, specification and code (β leaves Table 3, Algorithms 10 to 12 lose b0, B2' loses τβ/2, and Tables 1 and 4 and Lemma 4.3 are redone). The public-key encoding changes. Impact: public key 976 to 1456 bytes at CS-128, +49.2% (count); key generation and verification +0% to +8% at CS-128 (estimate). The finding evaluated CS-128 only; line 18 is the same at CS-256 and CS-512 (β = 128 and 32), where the same change is 1760 to 3104 bytes (+76.4%) and 4288 to 6208 bytes (+44.8%). No signature byte is added, and the secret key keeps its size, since it already stores both parts of b (`params.h:121`). Key generation and verification hash the longer key: 480 more bytes on about 6,900 and 6,200 bytes hashed or squeezed at CS-128, which is 7.0% and 7.8% if hashing were the whole operation and less otherwise; with the seed bytes of sign-07-4, key generation is 8.4% on the same count. Not costed: keeping the compression and covering c_r·b0 inside the rejection step. The shift per iteration on z2 would grow from τ' to τ'(1 + β/2), 17 times at CS-128, and masks that much wider do not fit below q/2 (judgment). Dropping fewer bits trades key bytes against that shift: the CS-128 key stays under +10% only with four of the five bits still dropped (1072 bytes, +9.8%), where the shift is 9 times.
- **sign-07-4.** Seed the expander with the whole seed and the nonce: `H_Init(&state, rho, SEEDBYTES + 2)` takes `HBYTES + 2` at `sampling.c:252`, in each of the six trees. The specification is sound (ExpandS derives a child seed per component, physical p. 9; ρ1 has 2λ bits, Algorithm 10 line 2). Scope: Single line, code. Impact: key generation +0% to +2% (estimate); signing and verification do not run ExpandS. Each of the ℓ + k calls already runs the whole sampler, and the only work that was skipped is absorbing the second half of the seed: 16 to 64 more bytes per call, which is 1.4%, 1.5% and 1.8% of the bytes that key generation hashes or squeezes at the three sets, and less of the whole operation.
- **sign-07-5.** Carry the message length as `size_t` and form the bit length in 64 bits: `int len` in `CS_Sign` and `CS_Verify` (`cs.c:65–68,151–154`) and the `H` macro (`symmetric.h:10`). The specification is sound (µ := H(tr, M), Algorithm 11 line 3). Scope: Function, code. Impact: none: the bytes hashed are the same for every message that does not overflow.
