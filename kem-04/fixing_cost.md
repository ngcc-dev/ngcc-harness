## Remediation

- **kem-04-2.** Derive the rejection key from the received ciphertext: `K(ξ, ct)` in place of `K(ξ, ct′)` in Algorithm 2 (physical p. 7), and `ct` in place of `check_ct` in the last call of `ccakem_decaps` (`src/common/ccakem.c:142`). This is the usual implicit rejection of the Fujisaki–Okamoto transform. It addresses the root cause that the report states, in all four sets: the key of an invalid ciphertext no longer depends on what the ciphertext decrypts to. Scope: Function, specification and code. Impact: none. The decapsulator already holds `ct`, the hash input keeps its length, and a valid ciphertext gives the same key as before because `ct′ = ct`. The report records no proposed fix. Not checked: whether the salt should also enter `K`; it would add 16 hashed bytes.
- **kem-04-3.** New parameters for BAG-Piglet-128; the other three sets are not affected as filed.
