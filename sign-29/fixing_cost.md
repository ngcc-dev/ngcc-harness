## Attacks considered

- sign-29-1: One signature reveals the complete signing witness (Design, Confirmed)
- sign-29-7: Public-salt GGM expansion exposes the complete signing witness (Implementation, Confirmed)

## Remediation

- **sign-29-1.** The root cause is that the masking polynomial P_δ takes its coefficients in the 12-bit subfield (Table 1, physical p. 4; §3.1 step 1, p. 6), while the published p_mid lies in F_(2^k). The costed repair samples δ_rnd, δ and δ_base in F_(2^k), the field of the polynomial that they mask. α_base, β_base and the evaluation points stay in F_(2^12). The report records the submitters' statement that sampling this polynomial over the subfield was a known risk taken for efficiency, and a mask drawn from the field of the masked polynomial is, in our reading, the usual choice in the TCitH framework that the specification builds on. The report records no proposed fix, and the proposal was withdrawn. Scope: Sub-algorithm, specification and code. ExpandSeeds outputs 2n − 4 + k bits in place of 2n − 4 + µ (Algorithm 8, p. 13), and δ changes field in Table 1, §3.1 and Algorithms 9, 10, 13 and 14, and in `CommitPoly`, `ComputePoly`, `ComputeEva` and `RecomputePolyProof`. No encoding changes. Impact on sizes: none, because p_mid already takes k bits per repetition in the size formula (p. 16), and δ is derived from the leaf seeds and never sent. Impact on signing and verification: each of the τN leaves expands k − 12 more generator bits (288 → 552, 544 → 1,060 and 1,056 → 2,088). In the submitted SM3 generator that is 1, 2 and 4 more compression calls per leaf, or +7.3%, +7.6% and +6.7% of all calls at Tins-128, -256 and -512 (estimate). Counted as bytes absorbed and produced it is +12.5% in each set. The repaired code also multiplies a k-bit δ_rnd by φ(i) in every leaf: 23, 44 and 87 subfield multiplications where the submitted code does one. That work is not in the percentages. Not checked: whether any other value needs the larger field, the soundness and zero-knowledge arguments at the new field, and the optimized and multi-core trees.
- **sign-29-7.** The specification is sound here: Algorithm 4 (p. 11) derives both children from the parent seed. The submitted `ChildNodeGen` receives the parent seed and does not use it (`Tins128/bavc_commit.c:109–124`; the Tins256 and Tins512 copies carry the comment "TODO: use encryption rather than XOF" at `:120`). The costed repair makes the two derivation calls depend on the parent seed. Scope: Function, code. Impact on sizes: none. Impact on work: the code skipped specified work, so this counts. The call count stays at two per internal node, and each call absorbs λ more bits. In the submitted `pseudoXOF` that crosses an SM3 block boundary at Tins-256 and Tins-512: 2 → 4 and 8 → 12 compression calls per internal node, or +7.6% and +6.7% of all calls, and nothing at Tins-128 (estimate). Counted as bytes it is +12% in each set. The specification's Encrypt-λ is an unnamed block cipher with a λ-bit key and block (p. 10), so its cost against the submitted hash calls cannot be counted. With a replacement XOF whose block holds the 2λ-bit input this repair adds no calls, and the answer would then rest on sign-29-1 alone: +7% by compression calls and +12.5% by bytes, before the added field multiplications.
