No: binding ã0 into the final challenge and moving the uBlockith-EM encryption witness by one block change no transmitted object, and the coefficient arithmetic that the corrected Vistrutith prover must add is estimated at 3% to 7% of signing at Vistrutith-d3-512f.

Impact: signing +3% to +7% at Vistrutith-d3-512f (estimate)
Scope: Sub-algorithm (large), specification and code
Assessed: 2026-11-23

## Attacks considered

- sign-05-1: The final Fiat–Shamir challenge omits ã0, allowing public-key-only universal forgery (Implementation, Confirmed)
- sign-05-4: uBlockith-EM witness misalignment reduces the specified relation to a two-round fixed point (Design, Probable)

## Remediation

- **sign-05-1.** Root cause: `hash_challenge_3_init` absorbs chall2 and the d − 1 transmitted coefficients and never ã0, although all three signing and verification algorithms hash ã0 … ãd−1. The costed repair is the submitters' proposal: absorb ã0 in signing and verification. Both sides already compute ã0 and it is not sent, so no size changes. For SM4th and uBlockith the scope is Function, code. The added work is λF/8 more bytes in every grinding hash, at most 3.3% of the bytes hashed per signature (SM4th-EM-d2-128f-lo) and at most +1.7% of signing with the hash shares of Figs. 5 to 9 (estimate). Counted in SM3 blocks instead of bytes, the largest increase is under 4% of the hash calls, at SM4th-d3-128f-ti. At Vistrutith the repair is larger, because the submitted prover skips specified work. It multiplies only the value and the tag of each commitment and hashes one tag from each of three constraint families as if they were (a0, a1, a2), where Vistrutith.OWFProve requires the three coefficient vectors of all constraints. The report records that honest Vistrutith signatures fail once ã0 is bound (sign-05-5), so the repair cannot keep correctness without that work. Scope at Vistrutith: Sub-algorithm, code (the prover side of the constraint arithmetic and the aggregation on both sides). Impact: the prover's GF(2^512) multiplications grow by 14% when values stay in GF(2^8) and only the missing coefficients are added. The prover is 14.7% of signing at Vistrutith-d3-512f in the optimized build and at most 43% in the reference build, which gives +2% to +6%, and +3% to +7% with the grinding-hash bytes (estimate). At Vistrutith-d3-512s the same repair is about +1% at most. Verification changes by 0.2%, which is none. A direct extension of the submitted code, which keeps its full-width value track and adds a third track, needs 34% more multiplications, or +5% to +15% of signing at 512f; it is not the costed repair because the first variant is cheaper. Not checked: whether honest SM4th signatures verify once ã0 is bound (the report's replays cover uBlockith and Vistrutith); the three further specification errata that the team lists; the 0.0.2 revision linked in the report, of which only the file list was read.
- **sign-05-4.** Root cause: the EM witness begins with S0, uBlockith.OWFConstraints takes the encryption witness from offset ℓke = 0, and uBlockith.EncCstrnts prepends S0 again, so the checked chain never reads `out`. The costed repair is the reporters' proposal: take the EM encryption witness after S0. Their alternative, not prepending `in`, gives the same chain. Scope: Function, specification and code: line 17 of uBlockith.OWFConstraints with the EM entries ℓke and ℓenc of Table 2, and the witness offset in the EM branch of the prover and the verifier. Impact: none. ℓ = 3072 and C = 3073 = 1 + 12·256 stay, the loop still checks 12 double rounds, and no transmitted object changes. The report records 5/5 valid signatures for a one-block offset with ã0 bound. Not checked: the written SM4th-EM relation, which the report records separately as sign-05-6 (Low).
