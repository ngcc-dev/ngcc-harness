The PKE secret `y`, decoded message `m`, fallback `sigma`, and shared secret are secret. The ciphertext and embedded public key are public. This review follows BRQC-128; the same decoder pattern occurs in the other reference parameter sets, and `gabidulin.c`, `qpoly.c`, `parsing.c` and `rbc_vec.c` are byte-identical in the optimized tree at all three levels. It is a source-level audit, not a measured remote attack.

Public parameter and length checks are likewise outside the secret-leakage claim.
