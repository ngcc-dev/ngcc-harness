## Remediation

- **sign-10-2.** Root cause: the derivative space of the public cubic map has dimension 165 of the 210 quadratic forms in r = 20 variables, and the missing 45 dimensions give the hidden X/Y separation by linear algebra; the quadratic space and a triangular flag follow, and public inversion reduces to f = m − n = 3 equations in three variables. This is a structural break of the trapdoor. The central map (X, Y) ↦ M_n((Q(X) + R(Y)) ⊗ Y) under the secret maps S and U (physical p. 5) was chosen so that the derivative route would not isolate that structure (§2.4 item 5, physical p. 20; §3.2.4, physical p. 35); the report shows that it still does. The repair is a central map whose public key does not expose the separation, which is a different trapdoor with a new analysis. Scope: Construction, specification and code. Impact: not bounded; the report records no proposed fix, so there are no figures to quote. A change of parameters alone is not costed. It would leave the stated root cause in place, since the separation is still recovered by linear algebra, and the report gives no cost model for the reduced system from which a larger f could be priced; at n = 10 the constraint m ≤ D = 2n − 1 = 19 (physical p. 6) also limits f to 9, against 15 and 30 in the two sets that the report says this route does not break. Not checked: the attack was not rerun, and ePrint 2026/2232 was not read.
- **sign-10-3.** Root cause: the target h = HashToField(FDSA-H || pkh || M) is fixed by the public key and the message (Algorithm 10 line 7 and Algorithm 11 line 11, physical pp. 12 and 14; `SIG_AlgorithmInstance.c:1996,2067`), and the target space F_q^m has 208 bits at Facto-DSA-128 and 992 bits at Facto-DSA-512, so a collision costs 2^104.23 and 2^496.22. The costed repair is the usual one for an unsalted target: the signer draws a salt for each signature, hashes it with pkh and M, and sends it; the verifier hashes the received salt. Scope: Function, specification and code (the hash step of Algorithms 10 and 11); the signature encoding changes from 2r bytes to 2r bytes plus the salt. Impact: signature +32.5% at Facto-DSA-128 and +9.4% at Facto-DSA-512 (estimate), for salts of 13 and 12 bytes. These lengths are the smallest for which the finding's collision, prepared for one guessed salt, costs the level per success against 2^80 signing queries; they are costing aids without margin, and a longer salt adds its bytes. Speed: none; the salt adds 13 bytes to one XOF input. Facto-DSA-256 has a 512-bit target space and a collision cost of 2^256.23, so the finding does not require the salt there. The alternative is to widen the target space instead, a Parameter set repair: m = 16 at Facto-DSA-128 gives 256 bits and public key +23.1%; Facto-DSA-512 needs m = 64, which needs n = 33 because m ≤ 2n − 1, and gives public key +13.1% and signature +3.1% (estimate; the sizes are exact for the smallest m that the report's formula puts at the level). Either way one object of Facto-DSA-128 grows by more than 10%. The report records no proposed fix. Not checked: a salt leaves the target space at 208 and 992 bits, and attacks on that space other than the report's collision are not costed; the signing success analysis at m = 16 (s = 3) was not redone.
